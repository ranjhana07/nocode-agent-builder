# Billing and plans

Every workspace is on one of three plans. The plan sets how many apps, published sites and workspace members you can have.

## Plans

- Free allows 3 apps, 1 published site and 2 members. Free sites are served from a staging style host name.
- Team allows 25 apps, 10 published sites and 20 members. Custom domains are included.
- Business allows unlimited apps and members and adds audit logs and single sign on.

## Upgrades and downgrades

Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated. Downgrades apply at the end of the billing period. When a downgrade would leave more published sites than the new plan allows, the oldest sites are unpublished first.

## Invoices

Invoices are sent to the billing contact on the first day of each month. The billing contact does not need to be a workspace member.

## Quick facts

- Apps allowed on the Free plan: 3
- Published sites allowed on the Team plan: 10
- Plan that adds audit logs: Business
- Invoice day of the month: 1
